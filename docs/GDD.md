# GDD.md

## Project Summary

Inner Shadow is a **top-down action RPG** focused on:
- responsive combat,
- small, dense levels,
- a core risk/reward system: Dark Essence.

This is a **solo project**.  
The goal is a **small, polished, complete game**, not a large RPG.

---

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. Player explores hostile area
2. Enters combat encounters
3. Uses abilities → generates Dark Essence
4. Gains power at higher Essence
5. Risks losing control at max Essence
6. Defeats enemies → progresses forward
7. Reaches boss encounter

Repeat in tighter, more challenging scenarios.

---

## Player

Name: Valerius

### Core abilities (initial set)
- Basic Attack
- Shadow Strike (single target damage)
- Shadow Shield (defensive)
- Shadow Bind (control / CC)

Abilities:
- always available
- generate Dark Essence
- may scale with Essence

---

## Dark Essence System

Core mechanic.

### Essence Behavior

- Generated by:
    - abilities
    - combat actions

- Does NOT function as mana

---

### Thresholds

0–40 → normal  
40–80 → empowered  
80–100 → unstable  
100 → frenzy

---

### Effects

Higher Essence may give:
- increased damage
- increased attack speed
- lifesteal
- stronger ability effects

---

### Risks

At high Essence:
- increased incoming damage
- instability effects (visual / gameplay)
- forced frenzy at 100

---

### Frenzy

At 100 Essence:
- temporary loss or reduction of control
- very high damage output
- ends with penalty (e.g. stun or exhaustion)

---

### Control Mechanics (optional)

Player may have:
- ability to reduce Essence
- ability to convert Essence into effects

---

## Enemies

Use simple archetypes:

### Melee
- fast, direct pressure

### Ranged
- distance threat

### Tank / Elite
- high HP, slow, strong

### Caster
- abilities, summons, disruption

Avoid redundant enemy types.

---

## Boss

Final encounter (Azmodan or equivalent).

Boss should test:
- positioning
- timing
- Essence control

Boss mechanics should interact with Dark Essence if possible.

---

## Levels

### Design rules

- small, dense areas
- no large empty spaces
- focus on combat encounters

---

### Structure (preferred)

1. Entry area
2. Combat encounters
3. Choke point / arena
4. Boss arena

---

### First Level (vertical slice target)

- 1 tileset (e.g. ruined city or forest)
- 3 enemy types
- 1 boss
- 20–40 minutes gameplay
